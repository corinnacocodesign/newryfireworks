The firework code

Respect the firework.
Protect the moment.

Fireworks are dangerous when instructions are ignored. Never return to a lit firework.

  1. Only buy fireworks from licensed retailers and check they meet current safety standards.
  2. Keep fireworks in a closed box. Remove them one at a time and close the box again.
  3. Never put fireworks in your pocket and never throw fireworks.
  4. Tell neighbours before your display, especially those with pets, children or elderly family members.
  5. Avoid setting fireworks off late at night, particularly on a school night.
  6. Make sure pets are safe and secure before the display starts.
  7. Read and follow the instructions on every individual firework.
  8. Light fireworks one at a time, at the end of the fuse and at arm’s length.
  9. Never return to a lit firework unless its instructions specifically advise otherwise.
  10. Light sparklers one at a time and wear suitable gloves.
  11. Never give sparklers to a child under five.
  12. Never throw spent fireworks onto a bonfire.
